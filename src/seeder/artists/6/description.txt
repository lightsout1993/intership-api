Ilya Efimovich Repin is perhaps one of the most recognised Russian painters not only in Russia but worldwide. Having lived for 86 years and rising to fame quite early, he undoubtedly followed a very long and fruitful path in both his career and personal life. Repin's works convey Russian reality and the social movements of his time with exceptional accuracy and awareness, setting and solving artistic goals that no other painters had previously attempted. From the very beginning of his artistic career he was recognized for his incredible talent and a century later Ilya Efimovich Repin is still known to us as a unique artist with a worldwide reputation.
